# espc-model-fields-repo

The US Navy ESPC-D-V02 scalar fields, as gridded data for the
[ocean map](https://oceansensing.org/visualization/): sea surface
temperature, salinity and height, sea ice concentration and thickness, the
temperature at 30 m and the ocean heat content (tropical cyclone heat
potential). The currents from the same model run are published by
`espc-model-repo`.

Updated three times an hour, when the model has a newer hour: the fields
three-hourly, the heat content six-hourly.
